All skills + how to get them

Non-negotiables

  • Warm up 5–10 min before every deep backbend, oversplit or tumble.
  • Handsprings and aerials: learn with a coach or spotter on mats. No solo attempts on hard floors.
  • Sharp pain, pinching in the lower back, or pain behind the knee → stop that skill for the day.
  • At least 1 easy day a week. Flexibility grows when you recover.
